# STORYBOARD — 《把回忆拼好给你》 · Piecing the memories together for you

**Song** 《把回忆拼好给你》 — cici_（词 Douji-T · 曲 Douji-T/Shingo.S · 中文词 Yvette；原曲《願い〜あの頃のキミへ〜》）
**Audio used** `assets/song.m4a` — the first **78.881 s** of 椰子水很甜's 纯享Beat (instrumental), cut on a bar line.
**Grid** 94.82 BPM, 4/4, beat = 0.632777 s, bar = 2.531109 s, first downbeat at **0.415 s** (`PROJECT.offset`).
Verified against the arrangement: the drums/bass drop is on bar 7 = 18.133 s (measured onset 18.135), the chorus
crash on bar 13 = 33.32 s. 31 bars, 24 fps, 960×540, 1893 frames.

**Logline** A small terracotta figure wants to give back the memories of a love that has ended, but they will
only come to him in pieces — so he pieces them together, lives inside the picture they make, and then folds it
into a paper plane and sends it out of the window into the dawn, keeping one small scrap for himself.

**World** One room, seen three times: at night (indigo walls, one warm lamp, a desk, a shoebox of torn paper,
an empty frame on the wall, the moon in the window); then the memories the paper opens into (a summer sea in
hard daylight; the same beach at festival night); then the same room at dawn.
**Palette arc** cold indigo + one lamp-gold → summer sky/sea/sand → festival indigo with gold, rose and teal
bursts → dawn peach and gold. The room's own colours come back at the end, warmed by the sunrise.

**Motif** Torn paper fragments with a pale ripped edge, and the stitch that joins them. They are: the scraps on
the desk, the sheet that swallows the room, the sparks of the fireworks, the plane at the end, and the one small
picture left in the frame that was empty in shot 1.

**Cast**

| who | look | rule |
|---|---|---|
| **我** | the kit's Clawd, terracotta, `u` 20–30 | the only thing that exists in the room, in the present |
| **你** | the same Clawd in pale moon-blue (`you()`) | **only ever inside a memory or a fragment. Never in the room, never now.** |

**我's emotion arc** sad → cry → thinking → surprised → determined → hopeful → love → starstruck → cry →
relieved → hopeful. The turn is at shot 12: he stops holding on and starts giving.

**The rule for transitions** every seam is paper: a tear wipe, a fragment over the lens, a match cut from the
painted sea to the real one, or the light itself carrying the cut. The drop at bar 7 is the one hard smash.
